About

I am a full-stack developer who turns product ideas into working software. I co-founded Web HAP Studio and run commercial projects from kickoff to production: interfaces, backend logic, APIs, deployment, and maintenance.

I like owning the whole path — talking to a client, gathering requirements, implementing the UI, writing the server, connecting third-party APIs, shipping on Vercel, checking performance in Lighthouse, and staying on after launch. If something is unclear, I ask and ship instead of hiding behind a ticket.

Day to day I work in JavaScript and TypeScript with React and Next.js, and in Python with Flask and Django. REST APIs, JSON, Git, GitHub, and mobile-first CSS are the default. I am not married to a stack: if the problem needs something new, I read the docs, look at the source, and start building.

A second track is Telegram: bots, Mini Apps, and small tools that live where people already spend time. I have shipped a Mini App game and a calculator bot, and I keep looking at how Telegram, web UI, and AI can sit in one product.

Claude, GPT, and Cursor are part of the engineering workflow — reading unfamiliar code, prototyping faster, refactoring, and debugging. The point is not to generate a page and walk away. I stay responsible for the result, the structure, and the details a user will actually click.

I am looking for a Middle+ full-stack or AI-adjacent role — remote, part-time, or full-time. The work I want is practical: AI-powered web apps, internal tools, SaaS, automation, Telegram products. If you have a messy problem and need someone who will take it to production, write to me.
